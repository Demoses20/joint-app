3. Diamond Wallet and transfers

Diamonds are platform credits and are not a bank deposit or cash unless a payout is expressly made available and approved under JOINT's payout rules. Check the recipient and amount before confirming a transfer. Transfers may be irreversible once completed. JOINT may apply a service charge or transfer fee at the rate displayed or configured by the platform administrator. The applicable fee and net recipient amount should be shown before confirmation. Never share your wallet PIN.

4. Purchases, refunds and payouts

Refunds, payout eligibility, processing times and fees depend on the applicable transaction and approved payout method. 5. Courses and assessments

Course content and assessment outcomes should be used for learning. AI-generated teaching or scoring may contain errors and may require teacher or administrator review. Certificates are issued only when the relevant course requirements have been met and verified.

6. Privacy and communications

JOINT processes account and activity information to provide, secure and improve its services, handle payments, support users and meet legal obligations. Your notification preferences can be managed in Settings. Do not include passwords, wallet PINs or sensitive payment credentials in support messages.

7. Availability and enforcement

Features may be updated, temporarily unavailable or discontinued. JOINT may restrict accounts or transactions that breach these terms, threaten users or compromise platform integrity, subject to applicable law.

8. Support and changes
