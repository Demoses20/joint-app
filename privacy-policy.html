Privacy Policy

Effective date: 23 November 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how JOINT ("JOINT", "we", "us" or "our") collects, uses, stores and shares information when you use the JOINT website, Android application and related services (together, the "Services"). It also explains how to contact us and request deletion of your account.

By using the Services, you acknowledge that you have read this Policy. Where applicable law requires consent for a particular processing activity, we will request that consent separately.

Privacy contact: technicaldemoses@gmail.com. You can use the form below to prepare a privacy or account-deletion request. The form opens your email application; it does not send information directly from this webpage.

1. Information we may collect

The information collected depends on the JOINT features you use and the permissions you grant. It may include:

  • Account and identity information: email address, account identifiers, username, display name, authentication information handled by our authentication provider, and information you add to your profile.
  • Profile and user content: profile details, posts, comments, messages, course or class content, meeting-related content, images, videos, files and other material you choose to submit or share.
  • Wallet and transaction information: Diamond Wallet balances, wallet transaction history, purchase and payout records, selected currency, payment references, and related fraud-prevention or verification information. We do not ask you to disclose your account password or wallet PIN to support staff.
  • Support and communications: support requests, messages sent to JOINT, and information needed to respond to you.
  • Device and technical information: device and app information, browser information, diagnostic events, log data, push-notification tokens and technical data needed to operate, secure and troubleshoot the Services.
  • Permissions and media: if you choose to use features that need them, camera, microphone, photo, video or file access. Android may ask for permission before the relevant feature is used.
  • Usage information: interactions with JOINT features, preferences and settings, and service activity needed to provide and improve the Services.

Do not upload sensitive personal information unless it is necessary for the feature and you are comfortable sharing it.

2. How we use information

We may use information to:

  • create, authenticate and maintain your JOINT account;
  • provide profiles, social features, posts, messaging, courses, meetings and other features you request;
  • operate the Diamond Wallet, process and verify payments, maintain transaction records and prevent fraud;
  • respond to support, privacy and account-deletion requests;
  • send service, security, account and transaction notifications;
  • protect users, enforce our terms, investigate abuse and maintain platform security;
  • diagnose errors, maintain and improve the Services; and
  • comply with applicable legal obligations and resolve disputes.

3. When we share information

We do not sell your personal information. We may share information only as needed for the purposes described in this Policy, including with:

  • Service providers: providers that support hosting, databases, authentication, storage, notifications, security and technical operations. JOINT's project is configured to use Supabase and Firebase services for some of these functions.
  • Payment providers: Paystack for eligible website payment and payout workflows, and Google Play for purchases made through the Android app where Google Play Billing applies. Payment providers process information under their own privacy terms.
  • Other users: information and content you choose to make public or share through social, messaging, course, meeting or other collaborative features may be visible to the intended audience or other users according to the feature's settings.
  • Authorities and other parties: where required by law, legal process, to protect rights and safety, or in connection with a business reorganization subject to appropriate safeguards.

We do not control the privacy practices of third-party services. Please review their policies before using their services.

4. Payments and Diamond Wallet

Website Diamond purchases may be processed through Paystack. Purchases made in the JOINT Android application are intended to use Google Play Billing. The relevant provider processes payment credentials and payment details. JOINT may receive transaction identifiers, payment status, amount, currency and related verification information so that it can confirm a transaction and update your wallet.

Never send your password, wallet PIN, one-time password or full payment-card details to JOINT support. JOINT support will not need your wallet PIN.

5. Permissions, notifications and location

Some features may request access to your camera, microphone, photos, videos, files or notifications. You can manage permissions in your device settings. If a feature uses location information, we will request or explain the relevant permission and purpose at the point of use. We do not state that every feature requires every permission.

6. Data retention and security

We keep information for as long as reasonably necessary to provide the Services, maintain account and transaction records, resolve disputes, enforce our agreements, prevent fraud and meet legal obligations. Retention periods depend on the type of information and why it is needed.

We use technical and organizational measures intended to protect information. No internet transmission or storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure. You are responsible for keeping your login credentials and wallet PIN confidential.

7. Your choices and rights

Depending on your location and applicable law, you may have rights to access, correct, delete or obtain a copy of personal information, object to or restrict certain processing, or withdraw consent where processing relies on consent. You can also manage device permissions and notification settings through your device.

To make a privacy request, contact technicaldemoses@gmail.com using the form below. We may ask you to verify control of your registered JOINT email address before acting on a request.

8. Account deletion

You can request deletion of your JOINT account by selecting Account deletion request in the contact form below or by emailing technicaldemoses@gmail.com from your registered JOINT email address.

For your protection, JOINT may verify that the request comes from the account holder before processing it. We will delete or de-identify personal information associated with the account where applicable, subject to information that must be retained for legal, security, fraud-prevention, dispute-resolution or financial-record purposes. Content already shared with other users, and records that must lawfully be retained, may not be removable in every circumstance.

Submitting a request does not itself immediately delete the account. JOINT will review the request and may contact you for verification or clarification.

9. Children's privacy

JOINT is not intended to be used by children in violation of applicable age requirements. If you believe a child has provided personal information inappropriately, contact us so we can review the matter.

10. International processing

JOINT and its service providers may process or store information in countries other than the country where you live. Where required, appropriate safeguards will be used for international transfers.

11. Changes to this Policy

We may update this Policy as JOINT's features, services or legal requirements change. We will update the effective date above and provide additional notice where required.

12. Contact JOINT about privacy

Use this form to prepare a privacy enquiry, correction request or account-deletion request. Enter the email address registered to your JOINT account so we can verify your request.

When you press the button, your device will open your email application with a message addressed to JOINT. Review and send that email to submit your request.

Email: technicaldemoses@gmail.com

This Policy is intended to describe JOINT's Services based on the features currently documented in the project. It should be reviewed and updated whenever data practices, service providers or app features change.
