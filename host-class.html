TEACH ON JOINT

How would you like to host?

Choose the hosting account that suits you. You can register as an individual teacher or as an organization.

👤
Individual educator

Host as a Person

For independent teachers, tutors, coaches, skilled professionals, and people who want to teach under their own name.

  • Register with your personal information.
  • Verify your identity using an accepted ID.
  • Your class appears under your name or username.
  • Host only one active class at a time.
  • Only you manage your individual class.
  • Offer paid classes or subscriptions when eligible.
Continue as a Person →
🏢
School or organization

Host as an Organization

For schools, skills-acquisition centres, training companies, academies, colleges, and other learning institutions.

  • Register the organization's official details.
  • Submit organization registration documents.
  • Verify an authorized representative.
  • Host multiple classes and courses.
  • Assign teachers and administrators to classes.
  • Let students choose courses under your organization.
Continue as an Organization →
